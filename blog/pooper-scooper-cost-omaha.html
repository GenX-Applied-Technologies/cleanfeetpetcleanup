What Does a Pooper Scooper Cost in Omaha?

Typical prices, what changes them, and exactly what we charge.

If you're tired of spending part of every weekend on poop patrol, the first question is usually simple: what does it actually cost to hire someone? Here's what pooper scooper services typically charge, what changes the price, and exactly what we charge here in Omaha.

Typical Pooper Scooper Prices

According to HomeGuide's cost guide, which tracks prices homeowners are quoted around the country:

  • Weekly service: about $15 to $30 per visit for one dog
  • Every other week: about $20 to $40 per visit
  • One-time or first cleanups: from $60 for a light pickup to $150 or more for a yard that's long overdue

Most companies bill by the month, so a weekly plan in that range works out to roughly $65 to $130 a month for one dog.

What Changes the Price

1. How Often You Get Service

More visits cost more per month, but often less per visit. HomeGuide points out that more frequent pickups keep the per-visit cost lower and help you avoid extra charges for heavy buildup. Every-other-week service sounds cheaper, but there's twice as much to pick up each time.

2. How Many Dogs You Have

More dogs means more waste and more time in the yard, so almost every company prices by the number of dogs.

3. The Condition of the Yard

If nobody has scooped in a while (hello, spring thaw), expect to pay for a one-time deep clean before regular service starts. After that, weekly visits keep the yard easy to maintain.

Weekly or Twice a Week?

Weekly service is the right fit for most homes. Twice-weekly is worth a look if you have several dogs, a smaller yard that fills up fast, or kids who are outside playing every day.
